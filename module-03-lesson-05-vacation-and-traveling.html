Transcription

Part 01

Daniel: Hey, River! Welcome back! How was your trip?

River: Hi, Daniel! Hi, George! It was amazing! I just came back from Bolivia. I went to the Uyuni Salt Flat and took hundreds of pictures. It was one of the most beautiful places I have ever seen.

George: That sounds incredible, River! I've always wanted to go there.

Part 02

George: Did you travel alone?

River: No, I didn't. I went with a group of friends. We met some really interesting people during the trip and we learned a lot about the local culture.

Daniel: Wow, River, that's great.

Part 03

Daniel: But honestly, I think I would be too tired. I prefer staying home during my vacation. Last month, I took a few days off, and I just stayed home. I organized my apartment and relaxed a lot.

River: Really, Daniel?

Part 04

River: Didn't you travel anywhere?

Daniel: No, I didn't. I just needed some rest. I usually work a lot, so staying home helps me recover. I watched series, cooked, and enjoyed my time alone.

George: I get you, Daniel.

Part 05

George: I like relaxing too. Actually, I like traveling by car and I'm planning my next trip now.

River: Oh really? Where will you go?

George: I'm going to visit my family next month. I will drive to a small town where my parents live. I also will visit some friends on the way.

Daniel: That sounds nice, George.

Part 06

Daniel: Traveling by car is comfortable.

George: Yes, exactly! I like it because I can stop whenever I want. Last time, I drove for hours, but I stopped in small towns and tried local food. It was a great experience.

River: That's cool, George.

Part 07

River: But don't you like big cities?

George: Not really, River. I think big cities are too crowded and sometimes dangerous. I prefer quiet places. On my next trip, I will stay in small towns and spend more time with family.

Daniel: I agree with George. Big cities can be stressful.

Part 08

Daniel: River, how was Bolivia? Was it crowded?

River: Not at all, Daniel. That's what I loved! The salt flat is huge and very calm. At night, we saw the stars, and the sky looked incredible. I felt very peaceful there.

George: That sounds amazing.

Daniel: Yeah, awesome.

Part 09

Daniel: River, after this trip, are you planning another one?

River: Not right now I'm still tired! This week, I'm just working and resting. But next year, I will travel again I want to explore another country.

George: I see. It makes sense.

Part 10

George: As for me, I will start my trip in two weeks. I'm already excited.

Daniel: Nice! Maybe I will travel one day. Maybe I will visit a calm and relaxing place.

River: You should try it, Daniel! At least once a year!

George: Yes, Daniel, it will be a nice experience!

Daniel: Maybe you're right.

River: Let's get back to work guys!

George: Let's do this!

Daniel: See you around people!
