Part 01

River: Hi Bill! Hi Matilda! What do you usually eat to stay healthy?

Bill: Hi River! Hi Matilda! I try to eat a balanced diet. I eat proteins, carbs, and fats in the right amounts. I don't eat too much junk food.

Matilda: That's great, Bill.

Part 02

Matilda: A balanced diet is very important. I also like whole foods. I eat fruits, vegetables, and grains every day.

River: Which fruits do you eat, Matilda?

Matilda: I eat bananas, apples, oranges, and sometimes papaya. I also eat vegetables like carrots, broccoli, tomatoes, and spinach.

Bill: That's nice, Matilda!

Part 03

Bill: I usually prepare grains. I prepare lentils, beans, and chickpeas. They are very nutritious.

River: Do you cook your meals at home, Bill?

Bill: Yes, I do. I prepare my food at home because it's healthier. I don't like processed meals. I avoid them. They are high in additives.

Matilda: I agree, Bill.

Part 04

Matilda: When I cook, I use whole grains like oats and whole flour. They are better than refined foods.

River: What about protein? What do you eat?

Bill: I eat eggs, chicken, fish, and sometimes meat. I don't eat too much pork. I avoid it.

Matilda: I eat chicken and fish too, and I also eat mushrooms. They are a great protein option.

Part 05

River: What about water? Do you drink enough water?

Bill: Yes, I do. I drink water all day. I don't drink much soda. Sometimes I drink juice. But I prefer water.

Matilda: Same here. Hydration is very important. If we don't drink water, our body doesn't work well.

River: Yeah, you're right, Matilda. I am going to drink more water every day.

Part 06

Bill: I will stop eating junk food during the week from now on.

Matilda: That's a good plan, Bill! I'm going to prepare more healthy meals at home.

Part 07

River: When are you going to start, Matilda?

Matilda: I'm going to start tomorrow. How about you, River?

River: I will start today! I am going to make a healthy dinner with vegetables and grains.

Bill: Sounds great!

Matilda: Yeah, definitely!
