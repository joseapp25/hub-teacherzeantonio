Full Audio

Transcription

Part 01

Officer: What's the purpose of your visit?

Tourist: Vacation.

Officer: Where are you coming from?

Tourist: From Brazil.

Part 02

Officer: How long will you be staying in the USA?

Tourist: Two weeks.

Officer: Where will you be staying?

Tourist: In New York City, at a hotel in Manhattan.

Part 03

Officer: Are you traveling alone?

Tourist: No.

Officer: Who are you traveling with?

Tourist: My husband.

Part 04

Officer: May I see your passport?

Tourist: Sure, here it is.

Officer: Do you have a visa?

Tourist: Yes, a tourist visa.

Officer: What kind of visa do you have?

Tourist: B2 visa.

Part 05

Officer: When did you arrive in the United States?

Tourist: This morning.

Officer: Have you ever been to the U.S. before?

Tourist: No, it's my first time.

Part 06

Officer: Do you have a return ticket?

Tourist: Yes, I do.

Officer: When is your return flight?

Tourist: On the 15th of December.

Part 07

Officer: Are you a student?

Tourist: No, I'm not.

Officer: What do you do for a living?

Tourist: I'm a teacher.

Officer: Where do you work?

Tourist: At a school in São Paulo.

Part 08

Officer: Are you visiting family or friends?

Tourist: No.

Officer: Do you have relatives or friends in the U.S.?

Tourist: No, I don't.

Part 09

Officer: What places are you planning to visit?

Tourist: New York, Washington D.C., and Boston.

Officer: How long do you plan to stay in each city?

Tourist: Around four or five days in each.

Part 10

Officer: Are you bringing any food, plants, or animals?

Tourist: No.

Officer: Are you carrying any fruits, vegetables, or meat?

Tourist: No, I'm not.

Part 11

Officer: Are you carrying more than ten thousand dollars in cash?

Tourist: No, much less.

Officer: Do you have any gifts or items for sale?

Tourist: Just some souvenirs.

Part 12

Officer: Are you bringing any alcohol or tobacco?

Tourist: No.

Officer: Are you bringing any restricted or prohibited items?

Tourist: No, nothing like that.

Part 13

Officer: Did you pack your bags yourself?

Tourist: Yes, I did.

Officer: Did anyone give you anything to bring into the country?

Tourist: No.

Officer: Are you carrying any weapons or sharp objects?

Tourist: No. Nothing like that.

Officer: Alright, welcome to the United States. Enjoy your stay.

Tourist: Thank you!
