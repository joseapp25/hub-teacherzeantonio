Part 01

Customer 01: Hello! What's that?

River: Hi hi! That's a Dango. It's a traditional Japanese sweet made from sweet rice flour.

Customer 01: Does it have a filling?

River: No, it doesn't. Dango is just colored rice dough.

Customer 01: What colors do you have?

River: We have pink, white, and green dango.

Customer 01: Is there any difference between them?

River: The white dango is served with sweet soy sauce.

Customer 01: How much is it?

River: It's 2 for a skewer.

Customer 01: I'll have one, please.

River: Good choice!

Part 02

Customer 02: Hello there! What are those?

River: Those are mochi.

Customer 02: What is mochi exactly?

River: Mochi is made from soft rice dough with a filling inside.

Customer 02: How much does it cost?

River: It's 5 each.

Customer 02: I'll take two strawberry brigadeiro mochi.

River: Perfect! Your total is 10 dollars.

River: Excellent choice!
